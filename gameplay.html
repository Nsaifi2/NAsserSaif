Original local simulation inspired by Fortnite island 7875-7934-3852. Floor counts, prices, physics, and rarity distribution are illustrative. This game runs offline and does not control Fortnite. This version focuses on jumps and rare collection; rebirth, brainrot leveling, and live events are not included.
