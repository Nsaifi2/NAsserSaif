## Applied to the game

`gameplay3d.html` translates the verified visual motifs into a real playable
third-person 3D staircase: colorful tiers, white tile surfaces, glowing blue
rings, trees, a rainbow, moving spiked logs, an upgrade booth, a bank pad, and
original collectible characters. It includes WASD movement, gravity, jumping,
solid stair fronts, landing, obstacle collisions, and a following/orbit camera.

Prototype choices remain illustrative: five tiers, four-unit step height,
upgrade prices, original character designs, log placement, guaranteed named
rare pickups, cash on delivery, and the bank/shop layout. The actual floor
count, exact topology, jump scaling, income rate, rarity odds, collectible
models, and obstacle behavior cannot be inferred from these images. The 3D
prototype does not implement multiplayer, rebirth, leveling, or live events.
